Ultimate Difficulty Scaling

AI — API Keys

Bring your own key. Pick a provider, paste your API key, and choose a model. The active slot is the one the difficulty tool's AI uses. For local models (Ollama, LM Studio, vLLM, llama.cpp…) choose Custom and paste the base URL, e.g. http://localhost:11434/v1. Keys are stored locally on your machine and are never sent anywhere except to the provider you choose.
