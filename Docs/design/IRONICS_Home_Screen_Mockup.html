Handoff Spec — IRONICS Home Screen (R98)

The first decision in the game, made before any lobby exists. Two doors, two products, two purposes. A LEAGUE PLAY player never sees a stake amount, because there isn't one to pick.

Why the doors are not colour-coded. §10.2 states chrome is the app's own furniture and is identical for every player; identity is resolved by tag through the registry. Giving STAKED its own hue would make chrome carry meaning that belongs to identity — the most drift-prone mistake in the system. The doors differ by density, motion rate and content, never by palette.

The blend rule, size-gated

Electric core + arc-violet rim. The rim is ON at ≥64px (wordmark lockup, door panels) and the mark goes core-dominant at ≤32px (wallet currency marks, nav glyphs, HUD watermark) — a rim on a 16px mark is noise, not identity. In this screen: doors and lockup carry the violet rim; the wallet marks and arrow glyphs are pure Electric with no rim.

Focus and input (CommonUI)

  • Initial focus: LEAGUE PLAY — the free door, and the majority path. Never default to the buy-in.
  • Focus order: League → Staked → Loadout → Store → Venues → Career → Settings → wallet “+”.
  • D-pad ←/→ moves between doors; ↓ drops to the footer nav; ↑ returns to the doors.
  • A / Enter commits; B / Esc is inert here — the home screen is the root, with nothing behind it.
  • Hover and focus are visually identical so a controller player and a mouse player see the same affordance.
  • Focus never rests on a non-actionable element; the wallet chips are readouts, not stops (the “+” is).

Content rules and edge cases

  • Naming is law: every player-facing string reads IRONICS. BAG MAN and AFL never appear.
  • Wallet: tabular numerals, thousands separators, abbreviate ≥1,000,000 as 1.2M. Zero balance still renders — never hide the chip.
  • Volts = 0: the chip stays and the “+” gains emphasis. The staked door is not disabled — Watts entry remains open, which is the entire point of the earned ladder.
  • Wallet unavailable (profile fetch pending): chips show a shimmer skeleton, never 0 — a wrong balance is worse than no balance on a surface that leads to a wager.
  • Staked unavailable (region gate / age gate / service down): staked door takes the disabled state with a one-line reason beneath the title. League stays live.
  • Long localised titles: door headings wrap to two lines by design; the type ramp steps down one stop before it truncates.
  • 21:9 / 32:9: doors hold a max-width and the gap grows; they never stretch past a 1.6 aspect or the split stops reading as two equal choices.
  • TV safe area: all content inside 5% title-safe; the footer nav is the first thing to lose padding.

Owed before build

  • Type ramp is unapproved. §4 of the style SSOT flags it as derived with no spec behind it. This mockup uses a system stack; the shipping display face is an open decision, not something to inherit from here.
  • Semantic colours are flagged for approval (§2.5) — none are used on this screen, deliberately.
  • Bolt lockup: final 2D UI texture and the 3D hero emblem integrate as produced; the inline mark here is a stand-in at the correct rim treatment.
