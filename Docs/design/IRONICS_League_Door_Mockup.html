Handoff — League Play door

The free door behind R98's home-screen split. Four axes, no stake control anywhere, and per-band population because §5 makes transparency a requirement rather than a nicety.

The neon treatment is display-only, and that is the legibility rule doing its job. §10.1 size-gates the accent — "a rim on a 16-pixel mark is noise rather than identity." The tube construction, bloom and hum apply to the LEAGUE PLAY headline and nothing else. Every control, label and count is flat white on glass. Bloom at UI size is what makes neon interfaces unreadable, so the reference look lives exactly where it costs nothing and reads as identity.

How the tube is built

Three stacked layers per glyph, which is what separates a real tube from a text-shadow glow:

LayerConstructionReads as
::before7px stroke, rgba(150,190,255,.085), 0.4px blurUnlit glass tube wall — gives the letterform physical body
base2.5px stroke, rgba(200,225,255,.30)Cool reflection along the tube
::after1.15px stroke, near-white + the bloom stackThe lit filament inside the glass

The bloom stack IS the blend rule rendered as light — white core → electric mid → arc-violet outermost:

RadiusColourRole
3–8pxwhite → #D2E6FFFilament core, carries crispness
17–34px#1E5AFFElectric — the LEAD, does the identity work
62px#1E5AFF @ 72%Falloff into the wall
96–150px#A855F7 @ 80% → 42%Arc-Violet RIM — accent on the outer edge only, never the core

Hum: two shallow dips over 5.2s on steps(1). A real tube is never perfectly steady — but any deeper and it reads as a broken sign rather than a live one.

UMG note: this is a material, not a font effect. Ship it as an SDF text material with the bloom stack as layered outlines, or as an authored texture for the fixed headline. Do not attempt the three-layer stack with stacked UMG TextBlocks — they will drift at different DPI.

Population — §5 is a requirement

  • Per band, never aggregate only. "A healthy total conceals a dead band, and the dead band is exactly the one a player needs warning about."
  • A cold band never looks like a busy one. Live = filled dot, Electric, pulsing. Warm = filled, tertiary blue, no pulse. Cold = hollow ring, no glow, tile at 62% opacity, italic label.
  • Estimates degrade honestly. A band with no data reads "Quiet · no estimate" — never an optimistic number.
  • This is the cheapest fragmentation mitigation available and is purely a rendering decision — it needs no matchmaking change.

Edge cases

  • All bands cold: the grid still renders every size with honest counts; Find match stays enabled. A player waiting deliberately is a different experience from the same wait imposed silently.
  • Population unavailable: dots go neutral and counts read "—". Never fabricate a number on a surface whose whole job is honesty about population.
  • Battle Royale selected: the size axis re-labels to BR brackets; MatchPlay sizes do not carry over.
  • Bots (R87): "bots fill remaining seats" is stated up front, not discovered mid-match. Bots are a League Play feature and must never appear on the staked side.

Owed

  • Type ramp still unapproved (IRONICS_UI_STYLE_SSOT §4). The tube geometry is tuned to a condensed grotesque; a different shipping face will need the stroke widths re-tuned.
  • Neon as material vs texture — needs a call from whoever owns UMG performance, since a full-screen bloom stack is not free on console.
  • BR bracket set for the size axis under Battle Royale is not specified here.
