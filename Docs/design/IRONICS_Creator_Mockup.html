C3 · BuildRoom — the builder

The preview is the product. Live preview is the spawned pawn on the blank chassis, never a stand-in, and a combat-range zoom state is mandatory — things that read at portrait distance vanish in play (SSOT §5.3). Nothing commits until SAVE, which is what makes experimentation costless.

The channel rail is chassis-dependent — X line shown. Body colour is absent from the X-line flow entirely: it is not offered, not greyed, not explained. M_AFL_Character declares TeamColor and never consumes it, so a body swatch on this chassis would be a control that does nothing — and a disabled control still asks the player to wonder why. The original line, whose mannequin master does consume it, keeps the body channel. This is what SSOT §7 means by the chassis determining the channel schema: not the same panel with a warning bolted on, but a different set of rows.
