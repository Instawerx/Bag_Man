THE LOAD-BEARING RULE — the inverse of the SSOTs. Tier 3 holds status and only status. No design decisions belong in this file. A design decision that surfaces while updating the tracker belongs in a Tier 2 SSOT under Docs/ssot/; the laws it must obey are Tier 1, Docs/DOCTRINE.md. If you find yourself explaining why something is built the way it is, you are writing in the wrong file.

State vocabulary — small and honest.

PROVEN watched in PIE on a controllable pawn. The only thing that counts as done, and the entry says what was watched. · COMMITTED on disk and pushed, not PIE-verified. · SCOPED designed, not built. · OPEN in flight or blocked. · ARCHIVED superseded, retained.

OPERATOR-REPORTED marks a claim resting on operator report rather than a git artifact. PIE leaves no commit — that distinction has to survive, or an operator's word silently becomes indistinguishable from evidence.
